The game starts at Color wizard. Choose an adventure with the illustrated Explorer, Wizard, and Master buttons. Each level keeps its garden while you play. After an experiment, Adjust mix lets your child remove and replace one scoop.

Hints stay optional. The first gives a clue about the color. The second shows one recipe, including repeated scoops. Different recipes that make the same shade also count.

This is a simplified paint playground. Orange, green, and purple contain equal amounts of their two starting colors. For example, 2 blue + 1 yellow and 1 blue + 2 green are equivalent here. Very close shades count too. Real paints can behave differently depending on pigment strength and amounts.

No timers, lost lives, accounts, ads, or tracking. Works offline as a single file. Spoken prompts use your browser’s voice, when available. Keyboard: Tab, then Enter or Space to tap.
